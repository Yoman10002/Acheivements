These are the recently made **Minecraft Datapacks** in **2026** for version *26.1.2*

# Buffed Dragon:
  * **Brief Description:** It is a simple Datapack, which Buffs the normal Ender dragon. Making it much harder than normal.
  * **Issues:** Unfortunately, **ALL end crystals have Cages spawned** even if they are **placed by a player**. Also it has been shown impossible at times,           and the latest version hasnt been tested.
  * **Health:** The Health of the dragon is increased to **500hp**.
  * **Random Chatter:** The dragon now randomly says stuff, **Unfortunately repeats MANY TIMES**
  * **Phases:** The Datapack introduces 2 phases for the ender dragon.
    * **Phase 1 Attacks:** 
      * **Cage Crystals:** All Crystals are **Caged**.
      * **Explosive Fireball:** Makes the fireballs explosive, and increasing damage.
      * **Minion Spawns:** Spawns 6 **Phantoms("Dragon Minions")**, constantly every **30s**.
